Roon — try the sample family

Sample family — made-up photos and story, so you can see how Roon feels.

This demo is fully self-contained: it runs entirely in your browser with baked-in sample data — nothing is uploaded, saved, or sent anywhere. Tap “Explore the sample family” inside to look around.
